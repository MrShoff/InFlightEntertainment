# In-Flight Entertainment

Something to do on the long flight-path rides between towns: **Chess Puzzles**, **Wordle** and **Connections**.

## Usage
- The window opens automatically when you take a flight path and closes when you land (your progress is kept).
- The **Auto-show in flight** switch in the top right turns that on/off (default: ON).
- `/ife` opens/closes the window any time. `/ife auto` flips the auto-show switch; `/ife reset` re-centres the window.
- Drag the title bar to move the window. Escape closes it.

## Games
- **Chess Puzzles** (chess.com-style rated puzzles): the opponent moves first, then you find the best line. Click-click or drag to move, legal-move dots, promotion picker, green check / red X feedback, Retry, Hint, Solution, Next/Skip. Your puzzle rating adjusts Elo-style, and new puzzles are picked near your rating. Alternative checkmates are accepted.
- **Wordle** (NYT-style): a daily word, six guesses, flip reveal, on-screen and physical keyboard, Hard Mode, statistics with guess distribution, plus unlimited **Practice** words that don't affect your stats. While Wordle is open, typing goes to the game; Escape and other keys still work normally.
- **Connections** (NYT-style): find four groups of four; Shuffle, Deselect All, Submit, four mistakes, "One away...", results grid. There's a daily puzzle, and you can also browse every puzzle or jump to a random one you haven't played.

## Install
Copy the `InFlightEntertainment` folder into your WoW Forever client's `Interface\AddOns` folder.
